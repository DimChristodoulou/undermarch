How to read it

The graph is generated from the other pages of this site — it doesn't hold any canon of its own. If something here disagrees with Established Canon, that page wins.

Two views

Web is the default: a free force layout where clusters form around whatever is most densely connected. Useful for finding out what a thing actually touches.

Depth pins every node to its band, so the graph reads top-to-bottom as the world does — surface and abstract concepts at the top, the Root at the bottom. Anything without a depth sits in the top row. This is the view that matches Map 1.

What it can't do yet

The connections here are conceptual — "built by," "orphaned by," "rung 3." They are not routes. The atlas can tell you the Weir connects several clusters, but it doesn't know which Mouth opens onto which passage, so it won't work as a navigation map until those adjacencies are decided. That's a separate pass, and it needs decisions from you first.
