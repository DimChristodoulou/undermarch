Events & Cosmology

Undertears
The ~20 openings connecting the surface to the deep — each 40+ km deep and roughly 40 km across. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Weeping
Folk name for the cataclysm. Not part of the Burial Age cycle — the two share an ultimate cause and nothing else. Proposed Canon → Atlas →

The Archipelago Model
The Undermarch is discrete regions, not one connected cave system. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Stillness
The fading enchantment protecting the insectoid homeland. Core mechanism confirmed; further detail proposed. Atlas →
Threshold / Shallows / Mid Reaches / Under Deeps / Root
The five depth bands. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Inverted Gradient
The Undermarch cools with depth past the Mid Reaches. GM only — nobody has measured it. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Root Entity
Whatever is draining the world's deep heat from below. Its episodic feeding is what writes the Burial Ages. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Burial Ages
The ~4,000-year cycle in which the Root's draw drives sustained volcanism, ash and agricultural collapse — decades to centuries of it, laying down hundreds of metres of cover. The last one emptied the surface. Nobody alive knows any of this. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Floor (the grey course, the drowned wood, the Old Ground)
The buried ash-and-tuff layer beneath all of Kethal — 300 m+ in basins, ~40 m on plains, nothing on stripped highlands. Reached only where geology exposes it. Everyone knows it is there; nobody has any explanation for it. Established Canon → Atlas →
Builders / Sunfled / Sealing Era / Aftermath
The four eras of the dead civilizations, deepest to shallowest, physically stratified by stacked Burial Ages. The stack is canon; what each era actually was is not. Established Canon → Atlas →

The Surface World

Kethal
The largest continent, ~15 million km², and the one the campaign happens on. Five nations, two mountain ranges, west-to-east winds, and the Fallwater. The Undertear Compact was founded here, at Xynnar. Established Canon → Atlas →
The First Risings / The Long Quarrels / The Asbour Wars / The Settlement of Lines
Kethal's continental history: settlement at two points ~3,700 years ago, centuries of undeclared fighting over the ashward plains, the exhausted stalemate over the Asbour, and the treaty ~600 years ago that fixed the five borders. Nothing older than 3,700 years survives as history. Established Canon → Atlas →
Obun
The smallest continent, ~5 million km². Far north, cold, poor soil; it has never fed itself. Three Undertears opened inside its territories. Established Canon → Atlas →
Naidrora / Aubannon / Emmux / Jomoth
The four continents that are names only. Nothing is built on any of them. Established Canon → Atlas →
Corrach-Nom
The dwarven and gnomish nation holding Obun's highland interior — about four fifths of the continent — and the most technically accomplished polity in the world. It cannot feed itself. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Nine Coastal Nations
Obun's nine unnamed coastal states. Arrived by sea ~3,500 years ago, found the interior held, and became trading states: grain in, Corrach-Nom metalwork out. Established Canon → Atlas →
Velmack
The largest and most balanced Kethali nation — strong standing military, moderate eastern agriculture, ports on both coasts. Holds the Varngate Gap, governs the Fallwater, and hosted the founding of the Compact at Xynnar. Established Canon → Atlas →
Brannock
Kethal's elven nation, holding forest west of the Red Peaks' northern arm and Dunthara, its desert, east of it — which is why all three elven lines are native to it. Its borders are the oldest fixed borders on the continent; foreign traffic enters through one licensed market and goes no further. Homeland or enclave is undecided. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Tenemrea Kingdom
Agricultural and commercial powerhouse producing ~60% of Kethal's food on the richest soil in the world. A hydraulic state: ash gives the fertility, the Red Peaks give the water, the crown gives the canals. Its grain feeds Corrach-Nom; its wine reaches noble courts. Very little military weight. Established Canon → Atlas →
Surlund
The southern nation, holding Kethal's southeastern mainland and the island chain beyond it. One of the two First Risings — the oldest settled ground on the continent, and the poorest of the five. Its wealth is entirely positional: it holds both shores of the Cassiran Strait. Established Canon → Atlas →
Cludonea
A major maritime force built on import and export. Its decisive advantage is elemental magic — Cludonean air and water work is the most advanced anywhere, and every merchant ship carries a wizard holding wind and current to schedule. Established Canon → Atlas →

Kethal — Geography

The Red Peaks
The young, active volcanic arc running north–south down northern Kethal, dividing Cludonea from Tenemrea. These are the mountains children are taught to watch. The name is for the iron-stained ash, not the rock. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Asbour Mountains
Old, folded and extinct, running east–west across the middle of Kethal. Worn down far enough that the ore sits exposed — the mountains you mine, against the Red Peaks you watch. Owned by nobody. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Varngate Gap
The lowland corridor between the two ranges — the only easy overland route between northern and southern Kethal. Held and taxed by Velmack from the fortress town of Varngate. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Cinder Belt
The dry, ash-loaded strip immediately east of the Red Peaks. Grazing, quarry and charcoal country — thinly held, and militarily important out of all proportion. Established Canon → Atlas →
Dunthara
Brannock's open desert, and the dry half of the nation — east of the Red Peaks' northern arm, in their rain shadow, with no eastern sea close enough to compensate. Its seat is Sirrach. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Ebon Summit
The largest volcano on Kethal, standing on the Red Peaks arc. The first of the continent's active cones to be named. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Cassiran Bay
The bay Kethal's eastern coast forms off Tenemrea — shoal and delta for hundreds of kilometres, with Cassira on the one scoured channel. Distinct from the Cassiran Strait, which lies further south. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Cassiran Strait
The passage between Kethal's southeastern mainland and Surlund's island chain — the only short route between the continent's coasts. Named for the Cassira grain run. Surlund holds both shores and tolls it; the alternative is rounding the tail in open ocean. Established Canon → Atlas →
Ashward and leeward
Kethal's winds run west to east. The Red Peaks take the rain on their western face and pass the ash east, which is why Tenemrea and eastern Velmack hold the best soil and the worst rainfall — and why Brannock has a desert. Established Canon → Atlas →

Kethal — Cities and Hubs

Xynnar
Velmack's capital, a coastal city on the Fallwater river. It issues the charters, decided not to dam the Fallwater, and is where the Undertear Compact was founded. Established Canon → Atlas →
Kessek Roads / Draymund / Holtmarch / Varngate
Velmack's western port, its eastern port, its ashward agricultural march, and the fortress town holding the Varngate Gap. Established Canon → Atlas →
Aurelane
Tenemrea's capital, on the eastern plain at the head of the canal system. Established Canon → Atlas →
Cassira
Tenemrea's great eastern port, on the one scoured channel in a coastline of shoal and delta. Every grain ship for Obun loads here, and dredging it is a permanent obligation of the crown. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Ombrian Hills
Volcanic footslopes on the Red Peaks' eastern side — the Tenemrean vineyards, the most valuable ground on the continent by area. Established Canon → Atlas →
Threnhaven / Ondris
Cludonea's capital and great western port, and its northern port — the terminus of the Brannock trade. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Calvyr Halls
Cludonea's air-and-water college. Every ship's wizard is trained here, and the state controls who graduates. Established Canon → Atlas →
Aelthorn / Sirrach / Duneth Gate
Brannock's forest seat, its desert seat in Dunthara, and the licensed border market — the only place foreigners are admitted. Established Canon → Atlas →
Ormsvik / Vessgard
Surlund's capital on the Cassiran Strait, where the toll is assessed; and the cliff country, where the Floor stands exposed above the water. Established Canon → Atlas →

Factions & Peoples

The Sounding Line
Global faction — scholars and surveyors mapping the Undermarch, drawn from every nation, with the host nation predominating at any given Undertear. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Undertear Compact
Global faction — multinational garrison force at Undertear frontiers, founded at Xynnar in Velmack, which is a live grievance among the other four Kethali nations. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Vigil of the Weeping
Global faction — old fringe religious order, medicine and burial rites. Established Canon → Atlas →
The mainstream Church
The orthodox authority that declared the Vigil heretical. Nothing else about it is built. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Corrach-Nom Diarchy
Corrach-Nom's government for the past thousand years: two crowns, always one dwarf and one gnome, alternating each reign, both elected by a council of a hundred drawn from the guilds and wed at coronation. The council may depose the pair, never one crown alone. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Diarchy's Quiet Unit (GM only)
Corrach-Nom's covert working of its own three Undertears for recoverable technique — and the reader of what its foreign delegates send home. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Deep Choir
Placeholder working name for the insectoid civilization. Proposed Canon → Atlas →
The Hushed
Isolated Aftermath-descended survivor people. Gameplay → Atlas →
The Surlund pilot guilds
The pilots who alone can run the Cassiran Strait's shoal, tidal, unmarked channels — as powerful as Surlund's crown and considerably older. How the two relate is unresolved. Established Canon → Atlas →

Goods & Agreements

Sounding lanterns
Lamps that burn without fuel and lean toward the nearest way to the surface. Universal below ground, and Corrach-Nom's invention rather than the Line's. Established Canon → Atlas →
Line cable
Cable that does not part under load. The Sounding Line's own work, and the Line has never shared it. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Lantern Concession
The Diarchy handed the Line the lantern schematics and took, instead of money, guaranteed grain tonnage and a permanent seat in Line survey operations at every Undertear on the planet. It was read at the time as generosity. Established Canon → Atlas →
Tenemrean wine
Grown on the Ombrian Hills' volcanic footslopes, and served in noble courts well beyond Kethal. Established Canon → Atlas →
The Strait toll
Surlund's levy on every hull through the Cassiran Strait, formalised ~300 years ago. Cludonea contested it, lost at sea, and has been paying since. Established Canon → Atlas →
