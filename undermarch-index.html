The UndermarchSplit by origin, not by topic — so you can review your own worldbuilding without wading through mine.

This site reorganises everything built so far into three tracks. It doesn't replace undermarch-bible.md/html, which stays the single comprehensive document with full reasoning, critiques, and section numbers — this is a faster way to see, at a glance, what's actually yours.

Currently in review: the Chapel Expansion — ten optional areas for Adventure 01, drafted for cutting. It sits outside the three tracks because it's temporary: once you've made your cuts, the survivors merge into Adventure 01 and that page goes away.

Start here

The Atlas is a graph of every named thing in the campaign — 125 entries, 259 connections between them. It cuts across all three tracks below, so it's the fastest way to find something when you don't remember which page it lives on, or to see what a change would break before you make it. Two views: a free web, or pinned to depth so it reads top-to-bottom like the world does.

How the split works

Every idea in this campaign came from one of two places: something you said, or something I proposed. That distinction is the entire organizing principle here — not lock status, not topic, not chronology.
