High Priority

  1. Who is the face of Velmack's crown at the Fallwater? Velmack governs the opening, but no named officer represents it — a royal warden, a governor, or a tax officer. Without one, the crown's authority is an abstraction the players never meet. Needed before session one. Established Canon →
  2. Ten Undertears or twenty? The Timeline and Common Knowledge both say roughly twenty openings, stated as something characters know. The player briefing says ten are publicly known and any number quoted is a floor rather than a count. One of the two is wrong. Established Canon →
  3. How many Undertears are on Kethal, and where? Three of the twenty are placed, in Obun. Kethal is the largest continent and holds the Fallwater, and nothing says how many more it holds. This blocks the continental map: every opening placed on the sheet drowns a watershed and changes the politics around it. Established Canon →
  4. Who is the Diarchy's delegate at the Fallwater? The lantern concession bought Corrach-Nom a permanent seat in Line survey operations at every Undertear, and the covert unit reads what those delegates send home. The Fallwater's delegate is a ready-made hook — the party finds a device, word spreads, and someone with an honest interest approaches them. Needs a name, and a decision on whether he knows what his reports feed. Established Canon →
  5. What is the entity at the Root? The level 17–20 endgame currently has no identity. Established Canon →
  6. What level does the campaign end at? Depends on how the clock resolves. Proposed Canon →
  7. Why is the giant wasp at the Drowned Chapel? Suggested, not decided: it came up Passage 26 toward the warmth. Adventure 01 →
  8. Does "Passage 26" mean the insectoids number side routes as well as the Undertears? 26 is more than the twenty Undertears, so it implies a catalogue of smaller routes to the surface. Not confirmed, and where Passage 26 goes below the drop is undetermined. Adventure 01 →
  9. Where did the B2a survey marker come from? A Sounding Line marker from a descent that is in no Line log. Its origin has never been decided; the leading option is a cautious-circle crew that reached the camp first and buried what it found. Adventure 01 →
  10. Species basis for the insectoids. Formian/Thriae proposed, never confirmed. Proposed Canon →

The Surface World

Kethal is now built — geography, continental timeline, five nations, settlements. What remains is listed here.

  1. Is Brannock the elven homeland, or an enclave? Still open. As a homeland, Brannock is confident and inward-facing and needs nothing from anyone. As an enclave on someone else's continent, its licensed-market posture becomes a survival strategy and it acquires an offstage relationship with a homeland elsewhere. The two produce very different politics, and the answer also decides whether all three elven lines being native is ordinary geography or a much stranger story. Established Canon →
  2. What do long-lived elves actually remember? Canon now states the Burial Ages are forgotten everywhere, with a single hedge — that elves may hold fragments closer to the truth, and that nothing in Brannock has been examined on the point. That hedge is either a door or a dead end and should be decided before anyone plays an ancient elf. Established Canon →
  3. Where does the Compact actually sit? It was founded at Xynnar, which is settled. Whether its permanent seat and courts stayed in Velmack or moved to neutral ground afterward is not. Leaving it at Xynnar sharpens the grievance; moving it defuses it. Established Canon →
  4. Surlund's pilot guilds versus its crown. Canon says the guilds are as powerful as the crown and considerably older. Nothing says how that relationship works, who collects the toll, or what happens when they disagree. Established Canon →
  5. The five active volcanoes. Kethal has at least five, most of them on the Red Peaks arc. None are named, and it has not been decided whether any sits deliberately off the arc as an unexplained outlier. Established Canon →
  6. The nine coastal nations of Obun. Unnamed. Trading states, arrived by sea ~3,500 years ago, dependent on Corrach-Nom's metal and on overseas grain. Established Canon →
  7. Which of the nine resents the Diarchy, and how far does it go? Canon says there has been no war in living memory and that factions exist who read the Diarchy's restraint as patience rather than good will. Nobody has been named and nothing has been done about it yet. Established Canon →
  8. Corrach-Nom's capital and the ports of the nine. Kethal's settlements are now named; Obun's are not. Established Canon →
  9. Five continents remain blank. Naidrora, Aubannon, Emmux and Jomoth are names only. Where the elven homeland sits, if it is not Brannock, is likely to be decided here.

  4. Did a Burial Age flatten the Petrified Forest? Every trunk fell inward and down in a single event, and the Burial Ages are now canon as the thing that ends civilizations through volcanism and ash — with the Floor's buried-timber horizon as the surface equivalent of exactly that. The connection is available and the POI text deliberately declines to answer it. Confirm the link, or keep it unanswered on purpose. Points of Interest →
